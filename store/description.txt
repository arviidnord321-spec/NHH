Grab your shovel and dig into a bright, bubbly world full of buried treasure and adorable pets! DIGBOUND: Pet Explorers is a cozy 3D digging adventure where every swing of your shovel can uncover something new.

Explore Meadow Camp, then break through the turf, loam and stone of real destructible dig sites. Use your Echo Scan to find hidden coin caches, gem geodes, fossils and rare relic shards, then hold to uncover them in a burst of confetti. Fill your backpack, sell your finds to the trader and spend your coins on better shovels, bigger backpacks, a pickaxe and a lantern.

Hatch eggs at the Egg Shrine to collect cute companions like the Pebble Pup, Sprout Bunny, Quartz Fox, Moss Turtle and the legendary Aurora Dragon. Your pets follow you everywhere, help you dig faster and give you special bonuses. Level them up, merge duplicates into Shinies and build your dream team of four.

Features:
- Smooth first-person digging in fully destructible 3D dig sites
- 4 biomes to unlock: Meadow Camp, Crystal Hollow, Ember Quarry and Aurora Depths
- 12 collectible pets with levels, bonuses and Shiny merges
- 4 themed eggs plus a free starter egg, each with a full 3D hatching reveal
- Echo Scan treasure hunting, quests, contracts, a museum and achievements
- Design your own explorer in the avatar designer
- Funky soundtrack, lively camp with friendly explorers, and automatic saving

How far down will your adventure take you?
